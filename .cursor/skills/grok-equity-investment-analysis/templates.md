## Final report skeleton

1. Scope and decision date
2. Decision summary (stance, MoS, max entry, IRR, gates, confidence)
3. Thesis and counter-thesis
4. Evidence and source ledger
5. Business, industry, segments, KPIs
6. Historical financials, accounting quality, cash flow, leverage, returns
7. Peer set and scored comparison
8. Forecast assumptions, probabilities, ±1 pp sensitivity, five-year scenarios
9. Cost of capital and valuation (FCFF vs owner cash flow)
10. Risk register, stress tests, catalysts, monitoring, invalidation
11. Open items and confidence
12. Quality-control self-audit
