# Saudi / Tadawul reference

Read this file when the issuer is listed on Tadawul (Saudi Exchange), incorporated in Saudi Arabia, or reports under KSA conventions. Apply in addition to the main skill — do not replace primary-evidence rules.

## Market and identity

- Confirm ticker on Saudi Exchange (Tadawul), share class, and whether the security is a common share, sukuk, or other instrument.
- Note free float, major shareholders (including Public Investment Fund or government-related entities), and related-party exposure when disclosed.
- Use Saudi Riyal (SAR) as reporting currency unless the filing states otherwise. If converting, cite FX source and date; the SAR is typically pegged to USD — do not invent a de-peg scenario without labeling it `[Estimated]` stress.

## Accounting and tax / zakat

- Many KSA issuers apply IFRS as adopted in Saudi Arabia; banks and insurers follow SAMA (Saudi Central Bank) rules and disclosures.
- Distinguish **zakat**, income tax, and mixed regimes. Do not treat zakat expense as identical to a corporate income-tax rate in WACC or residual-income models without stating the basis.
- For WACC/tax shields on non-financials: use the effective tax or zakat treatment actually applied to the cash flows being discounted; if unclear, mark `[Estimated]` and show sensitivity.
- Watch Shariah-compliant product mixes, especially for banks (CASA, financing, fee income) — keep conventional vs Islamic books distinct when disclosed.

## Banks and financials (common KSA case)

Prefer residual income / excess-return frameworks over operating-company DCF:

- Track financing growth, CASA mix and cost of funds, net special commission income (NSCI), fee income, cost-to-income ratio, cost of risk, coverage, NPL/Stage 2/3, liquidity (e.g. LDR), and capital adequacy (Tier 1 / total CAR) vs SAMA guidance.
- Cross-check return on tangible equity (ROTE), tangible book value, and dividends/distributions.
- Vision 2030 and mega-project exposure are context, not automatic growth — tie forecasts to disclosed pipelines, management guidance, and verified consensus only.

## Filings and sources

- Prefer issuer annual reports, reviewed interim statements, earnings presentations, and Tadawul/official releases.
- English and Arabic versions may differ in completeness; note which language/version was used.
- Secondary vendors (including Fiscal.ai) remain secondary to primary filings.

## Valuation hygiene for SAR names

- Match currency of cash flows and discount rates (SAR nominal with SAR nominal rates).
- Country risk premium: add only when justified and not already embedded in the risk-free rate or beta; disclose double-count checks.
- Peer sets: prefer regional banks/insurers/operating peers with similar mix; explain exclusions of global peers that trade on different funding or regulation.
